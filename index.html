Vacation Planner

Vacation Planner is a mobile Android app designed for frequent travelers. Vacation Planner allows users to track all of their vacations and excursions, and each of their related costs. Users are also able to search and filter against all of their data, and generate reports against their data for any reporting or analytical needs they may have. The app works fully offline and requires no account or internet connection so whenever they land at a new destination and have no internet - all of their trip details are readily available.
